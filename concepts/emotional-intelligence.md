# Emotional Intelligence (EQ)

> "What really matters for success, character, happiness and lifelong achievements is a definite set of emotional skills — your EQ — not just purely cognitive abilities." — Daniel Goleman

## One-Line Definition

The ability to perceive, understand, manage, and use emotions — in yourself and in others — to guide thinking and behaviour.

## Origin

The term was coined by psychologists **Peter Salovey** and **John Mayer** (1990) and popularised globally by **[Daniel Goleman](../people/daniel-goleman.md)** in his 1995 bestseller.

## Goleman's Five-Domain Model

| Domain | What It Means |
|--------|--------------|
| **Self-Awareness** | Recognising your own emotions and their effects in real time |
| **Self-Regulation** | Controlling disruptive impulses; staying calm under pressure |
| **Motivation** | Inner drive to achieve; resilience; optimism in the face of setbacks |
| **Empathy** | Reading others' emotions accurately; perspective-taking |
| **Social Skills** | Influencing, communicating, collaborating, resolving conflict |

## The Neuroscience: Amygdala Hijack

The **amygdala** (emotional threat-detection) can trigger a fear/anger response *before* the prefrontal cortex (rational thinking) gets the signal. This is the **amygdala hijack** — when emotion overrides reason.

- Hijacks last ~6 seconds (adrenaline flush)
- High EQ = faster recovery, less likelihood of escalation
- Trainable through mindfulness, journaling, coaching

## Why EQ Predicts Success

- Studies of star performers show EQ distinguishes high performers from average ones **more than IQ** in most roles
- In senior leadership roles, EQ accounts for **~85–90%** of the difference (Goleman's claim; some studies put it lower, but the direction is consistent)
- EQ is learnable — unlike IQ, it can be developed with deliberate practice

## Practical Implications

**For individuals:**
- Build a feelings vocabulary (naming emotions reduces amygdala activation)
- Pause before reacting (the 6-second rule)
- Seek feedback on blind spots
- Practice empathy through active listening

**For leaders:**
- Emotional contagion is real — the leader's mood infects the room
- Primal task of leadership: drive emotions in the right direction
- "Resonant leaders" attune to others; "dissonant leaders" create toxicity

**For teams:**
- Psychological safety = collective emotional intelligence in action
- High-EQ teams handle conflict constructively rather than avoiding it

## Critiques

- Goleman's original claims (EQ > IQ for success) were challenged as overstated
- Measurement is contested — is EQ a "real" intelligence or a set of personality traits?
- Some researchers (Locke, 2005) argued the concept is too broad to be meaningful
- The empirical evidence is strongest for **leadership** contexts; weaker for general life success
