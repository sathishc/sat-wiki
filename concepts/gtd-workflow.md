# GTD Workflow

David Allen's **Getting Things Done** (GTD) methodology — a complete system for capturing, clarifying, and engaging with work so that nothing falls through the cracks and the mind stays calm.

## The Five Steps

GTD moves everything that has your attention through a five-step workflow:

```
Capture → Clarify → Organise → Reflect → Engage
```

### 1. Capture
Collect every open loop into inboxes — physical trays, notebooks, digital apps. Everything that has your attention, nothing held in memory. The goal: as few inboxes as necessary, as many as needed.

### 2. Clarify
Process each item through a decision tree:
- **Not actionable?** → Trash it, file it as reference, or park it on [Someday/Maybe](./mind-like-water.md)
- **Actionable?** → What is the very next physical action?
  - **<2 minutes** → [Two-Minute Rule](./two-minute-rule.md): do it now
  - **Delegate** → add to Waiting For list
  - **Defer** → add to Next Actions list
  - **Multi-step outcome** → it's a Project; capture on Projects list with a next action

### 3. Organise
Route everything to the right list or location:
- **Calendar** — time-specific or day-specific commitments only
- **Next Actions lists** — by [context](./contexts-gtd.md) (`@phone`, `@computer`, `@errands`)
- **Projects list** — every multi-step desired outcome
- **Waiting For** — delegated items
- **Someday/Maybe** — deferred intentions
- **Reference files** — non-actionable information

### 4. Reflect
Review the system regularly. The **[Weekly Review](./weekly-review.md)** is the keystone habit: clear inboxes, review all lists, ensure every project has a next action. Without reflection, the system degrades and trust collapses.

### 5. Engage
Choose what to do. The system provides a complete, trusted inventory of all open loops. You choose based on context, time available, energy, and priorities — not on whatever is loudest at the moment.

## The Core Insight

> "Your mind is for having ideas, not holding them." — David Allen

Stress and anxiety about tasks come from **uncaptured open loops** — commitments rattling in the brain, draining attention even when not being worked on. By externalising everything into a [trusted system](./trusted-system.md), the mind is freed to reach [mind like water](./mind-like-water.md): calm, responsive, fully present.

## Why It Works

GTD is a **context-based** workflow, not a time-management system. Most productivity systems fail because they:
- Treat the calendar as a to-do list (everything gets scheduled)
- Conflate urgency with importance
- Skip the clarifying step (items sit in inboxes, un-processed)
- Have no reflection loop (lists stagnate)

GTD solves all four.

## Key Distinctions

| Common confusion | GTD clarifies |
|-----------------|---------------|
| To-do list | Context-organised Next Actions lists |
| Goal | Project (concrete multi-step outcome) |
| Inbox | Temporary capture — not a to-do list |
| Calendar | Hard landscape only (time/day-specific) |
