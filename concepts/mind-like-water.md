# Mind Like Water

A martial arts metaphor used by [David Allen](../people/david-allen.md) to describe the ideal mental state for knowledge work: calm, fully present, and responsive in exact proportion to the situation.

## The Metaphor

Throw a pebble into a still pond. The water responds with exactly the right ripples — not too much, not too little — and then returns to stillness. It doesn't over-react or under-react.

Most people's minds do neither. They over-react to minor interruptions (immediate full distraction) or under-react to important commitments (ignoring them until crisis). The culprit: **uncaptured open loops** — tasks, worries, and commitments living in the brain rather than an external [trusted system](./trusted-system.md).

## What Blocks It

Open loops create background cognitive noise:
- The undone task that surfaces while you're eating dinner
- The vague anxiety of "I'm forgetting something"
- The mental re-prioritisation happening in every meeting instead of full presence

Each open loop consumes a small slice of working memory and attention — even when not being consciously processed. Multiply by dozens or hundreds of open loops and the cumulative drain is enormous.

## How GTD Gets You There

Mind like water is the *outcome* of a functioning [GTD workflow](./gtd-workflow.md):

1. Every open loop is **captured** (not held in memory)
2. Every captured item is **clarified** (next action defined)
3. Everything lives in a **trusted system** (reviewed reliably)
4. The **weekly review** keeps the system current

When you trust that nothing is being forgotten — because the system holds it all — the background monitoring process shuts off. The mind goes quiet.

## Relationship to Flow

[Flow](./flow-optimal-experience.md) (Csikszentmihalyi) requires single-pointed focus with no competing demands on attention. Mind like water is the precondition for flow in knowledge work: you can't enter flow if part of your brain is running a background to-do scanner.

GTD and flow are complementary operating layers:
- **GTD** (mind like water) — the operating system that clears the cognitive decks
- **[Deep Work](./deep-work.md)** (Newport) / **Flow** — the application that runs on those clear decks

## Relationship to Calm

Allen distinguishes mind like water from *emptiness*. It's not about having nothing to do — it's about being **appropriately engaged** with whatever is in front of you, without the drag of everything else.

> "You can do anything, but not everything." — David Allen
