# Self-Awareness

## One-Line Definition

The capacity to recognise your own emotions, thoughts, strengths, weaknesses, and how they affect others — in real time.

## In Goleman's EQ Model

Self-Awareness is the **first and foundational domain** of [Emotional Intelligence](../concepts/emotional-intelligence.md). Without it, the other four domains have no grounding.

### Components

- **Emotional self-awareness** — knowing what you're feeling and why
- **Accurate self-assessment** — honest inventory of strengths and limits
- **Self-confidence** — a strong, realistic sense of your own worth and capabilities

## Why It's the Foundation

You can't regulate what you can't recognise. People with low self-awareness:
- React instead of respond
- Have blind spots that undermine their effectiveness
- Receive feedback defensively (it doesn't match their self-model)

High self-awareness enables:
- Better decisions (emotions as data, not noise)
- Faster recovery from setbacks
- More authentic leadership

## How to Build It

- **Journaling** — forces slow thinking about emotional patterns
- **360-degree feedback** — reveals blind spots others see
- **Mindfulness practice** — trains moment-to-moment emotional noticing
- **Naming emotions** — the act of labelling ("I'm frustrated") reduces amygdala activation (affective labelling)
