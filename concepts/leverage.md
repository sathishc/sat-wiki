# Leverage

The force multiplier on [specific knowledge](./specific-knowledge.md). Leverage is what separates earning linearly (trading time for money) from earning exponentially (output decoupled from hours worked).

## Three Forms

### 1. Labour
- Other people working for you
- Oldest and most well-understood form
- Requires managing people — costly in attention and trust
- Does not scale indefinitely

### 2. Capital
- Money working for you
- Highly scalable but requires capital to access capital
- The leverage of the old rich — financiers, industrialists

### 3. Code & Media ⭐
- Products with **zero marginal cost of replication**
- Write once, distribute infinitely
- **Permissionless** — you do not need anyone's approval to publish software or content
- The defining leverage of the modern era

> "Code and media are permissionless leverage. They're the leverage behind the newly rich. You can create software and media that works for you while you sleep."
> — [Naval Ravikant](../people/naval-ravikant.md)

## Why Permissionless Matters

Labour leverage requires convincing someone to work for you. Capital leverage requires convincing someone to invest in you. Code and media require only your own effort — and the internet distributes the output for free.

This is the key insight behind why a solo developer or writer can generate enormous value: they have leverage that historically required large organisations.

## Leverage + Specific Knowledge

The formula Naval describes:
- **Specific knowledge** → what makes you valuable
- **Accountability** → what earns you trust and reputation
- **Leverage** → what multiplies your output

Without leverage, specific knowledge earns you a high salary. With leverage, it can build wealth.
