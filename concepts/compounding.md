# Compounding

Compounding applies far beyond finance. [Naval Ravikant](../people/naval-ravikant.md) treats it as a universal law that rewards long-term thinking in all domains.

## Domains Where It Applies

| Domain | What Compounds |
|---|---|
| **Wealth** | Capital → returns → more capital |
| **Knowledge** | Understanding → better questions → deeper understanding |
| **Reputation** | Trust built over time → more opportunities → more trust |
| **Relationships** | History + goodwill → deeper collaboration → better outcomes |
| **Skills** | Practice → capability → ability to practise at higher levels |

## The Key Insight

Short-term thinking destroys compounding. Switching strategies, burning relationships, or cashing out early all forfeit the exponential tail.

> "Play long-term games with long-term people."
> — [Naval Ravikant](../people/naval-ravikant.md)

## Practical Implications

- Choose industries and problems where the knowledge is durable, not quickly obsolete
- Be consistent and reliable — reputation compounds through repeated interaction
- Avoid zero-sum games — they have no compounding because gains require losses
- The best investments (of time or money) often look boring for a long time, then suddenly obvious
