# Empathy

## One-Line Definition

The ability to sense, understand, and share the feelings of others — reading emotional signals accurately and responding appropriately.

## In Goleman's EQ Model

Fourth domain of [Emotional Intelligence](../concepts/emotional-intelligence.md). The bridge from self to others.

### Three Types (Goleman + others)

| Type | Description |
|------|-------------|
| **Cognitive empathy** | Understanding someone's perspective intellectually ("I know how you see this") |
| **Emotional empathy** | Feeling what they feel — resonating with their emotional state |
| **Empathic concern** | Caring about their wellbeing and being moved to help |

Great leaders typically use all three — and know when each is appropriate.

## Why It's a Leadership Skill

- Empathic leaders retain talent — people stay where they feel understood
- Empathy enables effective feedback (you can time and frame it well)
- In negotiations, empathy gives you more information about the other side
- Cross-cultural empathy is increasingly critical in global organisations

## Common Misconception

Empathy ≠ agreement. You can fully understand someone's perspective and still disagree with their conclusion. Empathy is about *understanding*, not capitulation.
