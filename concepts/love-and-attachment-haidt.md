# Love and Attachment

## One-Line Definition

Haidt's synthesis of attachment theory and positive psychology: secure, close relationships are not a luxury but a **biological necessity** — the single most consistent predictor of human happiness and wellbeing.

## The Argument

Drawing on John Bowlby's attachment theory and decades of wellbeing research, Haidt argues that the **need to belong** is as fundamental as the need for food or shelter. It is not a nice-to-have:

- Social connection is the strongest and most consistent predictor of wellbeing across cultures
- Loneliness and social exclusion activate the same neural circuits as physical pain
- The Harvard Study of Adult Development (75 years, thousands of participants) concluded: **good relationships — more than wealth, fame, or achievement — keep us happier and healthier**

## Styles of Attachment

Following Bowlby and Mary Ainsworth, Haidt notes that adults carry attachment styles formed in childhood:

| Style | Description | Effect on adult relationships |
|---|---|---|
| **Secure** | Comfortable with closeness and interdependence | Stable, trusting, resilient relationships |
| **Anxious/Preoccupied** | Craves closeness; fears abandonment | Clingy, reactive, easily threatened |
| **Avoidant/Dismissive** | Discomfort with closeness; values independence | Distant, suppresses needs, avoids vulnerability |

These styles are not fixed — they can shift with experience, therapy, and secure relationships in adulthood.

## Love as Multiple Phenomena

Haidt distinguishes between types of love that are often conflated:

- **Passionate love** — intense, obsessive, neurochemically similar to addiction; typically fades within 1–2 years
- **Companionate love** — warm, affectionate, enduring; builds over time; the foundation of lasting partnerships
- **Attachment** — the secure base; the person you turn to when threatened or overwhelmed

Mistaking passionate love for the whole of love is one of the most common causes of relationship dissatisfaction. Companionate love is quieter but more durable and ultimately more valuable.

## The Conditions Implication

In the [Happiness Formula](../concepts/happiness-formula-haidt.md), the quality of close relationships is the highest-[Leverage](leverage.md) **Conditions (C)** variable — more predictive than income, status, or material circumstances.

**Investment in relationships is investment in the highest-return happiness asset.**
