# Secrets

## One-Line Definition

A **secret** (in Thiel's framework) is an important truth that most people don't believe yet — the source of every great company and every contrarian opportunity.

## The Argument

> "Every great business is built around a secret that's hidden from the outside. A great company is a conspiracy to change the world."

Thiel observes that many people believe there are no more secrets — that everything important has already been discovered, mapped, and competed over. He calls this the **"End of Secrets"** fallacy, and traces it to:

- The **Unabomber** (extreme: technology is done, only destruction remains)
- **Economists** (efficient markets have priced in all information)
- **Conventional wisdom** (if something were valuable, someone would have done it already)

Thiel's counter: *the world is vastly under-explored.* The frontier isn't closed — most people just stopped looking.

## Two Types of Secrets

| Type | Description | Example |
|------|-------------|---------|
| **Secrets about nature** | Facts about the world not yet discovered | New physics, undiscovered biology, geography |
| **Secrets about people** | Things people don't know about themselves or hide from others | "People want X but won't admit it"; what people *do* vs. what they *say* |

Most startup secrets are of the second type. *"What valuable company is nobody building?"* Often the answer is hidden in human behaviour, not in nature.

## The Conspiracy Frame

> "A startup is a conspiracy to change the world."

You can't tell everyone your secret, because then it's not a secret. You tell your founding team. You work in relative obscurity. When you emerge, you've built something others can't easily replicate because they didn't believe the secret was true.

## Practical Test

Thiel's question: **"What do you believe that very few others agree with?"**

- Bad answer: "Our team is amazing / our technology is better"
- Good answer: A specific, verifiable claim about the world that, if true, justifies building what you're building
