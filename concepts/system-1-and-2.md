# System 1 and System 2

[Kahneman](../people/daniel-kahneman.md)'s organising framework for how the mind works. Two modes of thinking operate simultaneously, with fundamentally different properties.

> "System 1 operates automatically and quickly, with little or no effort and no sense of voluntary control. System 2 allocates attention to the effortful mental activities that demand it."

## The Two Systems

**System 1 — Fast thinking:**
- Automatic, unconscious, always on
- Pattern-matching from experience and memory
- Emotional, associative, narrative
- Processes in parallel
- Produces impressions, intuitions, intentions, feelings
- Cannot be switched off voluntarily

**System 2 — Slow thinking:**
- Deliberate, conscious, effortful
- Rule-following, logical, analytical
- Serial (one thing at a time)
- Requires attention; degrades under cognitive load
- Can override System 1 — but rarely bothers

## The Critical Insight: System 2 Is Lazy

System 1 runs constantly and generates answers continuously. System 2 monitors those answers — but only loosely. It accepts System 1's outputs unless there is a compelling reason to intervene.

This is not a flaw — it is efficient. System 2 is expensive. Running it constantly would be exhausting and slow. But the laziness means:

- Most decisions are made by System 1 without conscious review
- System 2 is often engaged only to *rationalise* System 1's conclusions after the fact
- Cognitive load, fatigue, or distraction reduce System 2's availability — and biases increase

## When Each System Excels

**System 1 is reliable when:**
- The environment is regular and predictable (chess, radiological diagnosis, fire-fighting)
- There is rapid, unambiguous feedback (so patterns can be learned)
- The domain has been practised extensively

**System 1 is unreliable when:**
- The environment is irregular or random (stock markets, political forecasting)
- Feedback is delayed, absent, or misleading
- The situation is novel (no learned patterns to match)

**The key condition for valid intuition:** The expert must have operated in a domain where the world is regular enough to make patterns learnable, AND received enough rapid feedback to actually learn them. Kahneman calls this "skilled intuition" vs "illusions of validity."

## System 2 Disciplines

You can train yourself to engage System 2 more reliably:
- **Pre-mortems** — before committing, imagine the project has failed. What went wrong? (Forces System 2 consideration of negatives System 1 suppressed)
- **Reference class forecasting** — instead of constructing a plan (System 1's inside view), look at base rates for similar projects (System 2's outside view)
- **Checklists** — encode System 2 reasoning into habits; expert checklist = System 2 made automatic
- **Algorithms** — Kahneman's finding: simple linear models consistently outperform expert intuition in complex, noisy environments

## Application Across the Wiki

**[Dalio](../people/ray-dalio.md)'s principles = encoded System 2:**
Writing explicit principles and applying them consistently is a mechanism for running System 2 in situations where System 1 would otherwise take over. The [idea meritocracy](./idea-meritocracy.md) is a System 2 institutional structure.

**[Hazlitt](../people/henry-hazlitt.md)'s one lesson = System 2 discipline:**
"Trace all effects for all groups" is an explicit System 2 instruction to override System 1's default of seeing only the immediate, visible effect.

**[Naval](../people/naval-ravikant.md)'s [judgment](./mental-models.md):**
Naval's emphasis on building judgment through [mental models](./mental-models.md) and [specific knowledge](./specific-knowledge.md) is the practice of making better System 2 tools — so that when the stakes are high, you have frameworks to reason with.

**[Reinertsen](../people/donald-reinertsen.md)'s flow principles:**
[Cost of Delay](./cost-of-delay.md) is a System 2 tool for overriding System 1's tendency to prioritise by gut feel, recency, or loudness of the requester.

**[Amazon LP](./amazon-leadership-principles.md) "Are Right, A Lot":**
> "They seek diverse perspectives and work to disconfirm their beliefs."
This is a System 2 instruction: actively seek evidence that your System 1 story is wrong.
