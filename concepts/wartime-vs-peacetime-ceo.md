# Wartime CEO vs Peacetime CEO

> "Peacetime CEO knows that proper protocol leads to winning. Wartime CEO violates protocol in order to win." — Ben Horowitz

## One-Line Definition

Two fundamentally different modes of leadership required by two fundamentally different business contexts — and most management advice only covers one of them.

## The Distinction

| | Peacetime CEO | Wartime CEO |
|---|---|---|
| **Context** | Clear path to market leadership; expanding opportunity | Existential threat; survival in question |
| **Style** | Collaborative, consensus-building, inclusive | Decisive, directive, single-minded |
| **Culture** | Encourages debate and broad input | Cuts off discussion; executes fast |
| **Priority** | Optimise and expand | Survive and focus |
| **Communication** | Open; explain reasoning | Command; no time to explain |
| **Mistakes** | Tolerates learning experiences | Zero tolerance for the critical ones |
| **Examples** | Bill Campbell (early Apple), Eric Schmidt (Google growth) | Andy Grove (Intel memory crisis), Steve Jobs (Apple 1997) |

## Why It Matters

Most management books, MBA curricula, and executive coaches are **peacetime manuals**. They teach:
- How to build consensus
- How to give feedback constructively
- How to create empowering cultures
- How to set OKRs

These are peacetime tools. In wartime, they're not just useless — they're actively dangerous. A wartime CEO who runs a "let's explore this together" offsite while the company is burning is failing.

## The Trap

The trap is using **peacetime behaviours in wartime** — which feels civilised but is slow, unfocused, and potentially fatal. Horowitz's examples from his own experience at Loudcloud/Opsware:
- The dot-com crash (2001) = wartime
- The years building toward HP acquisition = peacetime
- *He had to behave very differently in each phase.*

## The Reverse Trap

Wartime behaviours in peacetime also fail. A CEO who operates in permanent crisis mode:
- Burns out the team
- Can't attract or retain great people
- Prevents the culture-building that peacetime requires

## Goleman Connection

Horowitz's framework maps cleanly onto [Daniel Goleman's](../people/daniel-goleman.md) six leadership styles:

- **Peacetime** → Visionary, Coaching, Affiliative, Democratic styles dominate
- **Wartime** → Commanding, Pacesetting styles dominate

The best leaders are fluent in both.
