# David Allen

American productivity consultant and author. Best known for the **Getting Things Done** (GTD) methodology, one of the most influential personal productivity systems of the 21st century.

## Background

Allen spent decades as a management consultant before systematising his approach to personal organisation in *Getting Things Done* (2001). The book was revised and updated in 2015. He has worked with senior executives and organisations worldwide.

His work sits at the intersection of cognitive science (how the brain handles open loops), behavioural psychology (habits and systems), and practical workflow design.

## Core Ideas

Allen's central insight is deceptively simple: **the brain is for having ideas, not storing them**. Every commitment held in memory — rather than in an external system — consumes cognitive bandwidth and generates low-level anxiety.

The [GTD workflow](../concepts/gtd-workflow.md) externalises everything into a [trusted system](../concepts/trusted-system.md), enabling the brain to reach [mind like water](../concepts/mind-like-water.md): calm, fully present, responsive in proportion to the situation.

## Major Works

- **Getting Things Done** (2001, revised 2015) — the GTD methodology
- **Ready for Anything** (2003) — essays on GTD principles
- **Making It All Work** (2008) — GTD applied to self-management and control

## Key Concepts

- [GTD Workflow](../concepts/gtd-workflow.md) — capture → clarify → organise → reflect → engage
- [Mind Like Water](../concepts/mind-like-water.md) — the target mental state
- [Trusted System](../concepts/trusted-system.md) — the external repository for all open loops
- [Natural Planning Model](../concepts/natural-planning-model.md) — how the brain plans effectively
- [Weekly Review](../concepts/weekly-review.md) — the keystone GTD habit
- [Two-Minute Rule](../concepts/two-minute-rule.md) — if it takes <2 minutes, do it now
- [Contexts (GTD)](../concepts/contexts-gtd.md) — organising next actions by situation

## Relationships to Other Thinkers

- **[Cal Newport](./cal-newport.md)** — Newport's [deep work](../concepts/deep-work.md) is the application layer that runs on GTD's operating system. Newport explicitly credits GTD as necessary infrastructure for deep work.
- **[James Clear](./james-clear.md)** — Clear's [habit loop](../concepts/habit-loop.md) explains *how* GTD habits form; Allen's system explains *what* habits to form.
- **[Mihaly Csikszentmihalyi](./mihaly-csikszentmihalyi.md)** — [Flow](../concepts/flow-optimal-experience.md) requires the focused, distraction-free state that GTD (mind like water) enables.
- **[Ray Dalio](./ray-dalio.md)** — Both Allen and Dalio emphasise external systems over memory/intuition alone. Dalio's [principles](../sources/dalio-principles.md) are a trusted system for decisions; Allen's GTD is a trusted system for actions.
