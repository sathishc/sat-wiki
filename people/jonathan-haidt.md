# Jonathan Haidt

> "The mind is divided, like a rider on an elephant, and the rider's job is to serve the elephant."

## Who He Is

Jonathan Haidt (b. 1963) is an American social psychologist and professor at NYU Stern School of Business. He works at the intersection of moral psychology, positive psychology, and political psychology.

His two most influential books:

- ***The Happiness Hypothesis*** (2006) — a synthesis of ancient wisdom and modern psychology; ten "great ideas" about happiness tested against contemporary science
- ***The Righteous Mind*** (2012) — a moral psychology framework explaining why good people are divided by politics and religion

He is also co-founder of [Heterodox Academy](https://heterodoxacademy.org) and author of *The Coddling of the American Mind* (2018, with Greg Lukianoff).

## Core Philosophy

Haidt's worldview has two pillars:

1. **The divided self** — the rational mind (rider) does not control behaviour; it narrates and justifies what the emotional/intuitive mind (elephant) does. Most of what we think is reasoning is actually post-hoc rationalisation.
2. **Ancient wisdom + modern science** — the great philosophical and religious traditions discovered real truths about human flourishing; modern psychology can validate, refine, and extend them.

## Key Frameworks

- **[The Happiness Hypothesis](../concepts/happiness-hypothesis.md)** — the umbrella framework: ten insights about the conditions for a good life
- **[The Rider and the Elephant](../concepts/rider-and-elephant.md)** — the divided self; the limits of conscious reason; how change actually works
- **[The Happiness Formula: H = S + C + V](../concepts/happiness-formula-haidt.md)** — set point + conditions + voluntary activities; the three levers of wellbeing
- **[Adversarial Growth](../concepts/adversarial-growth.md)** — why some struggle produces wisdom and resilience (post-traumatic growth)
- **[Social and Vital Engagement](../concepts/vital-engagement.md)** — the two components of a flourishing life: flow (absorbed challenge) + meaning (purpose beyond self)
- **[Love and Attachment](../concepts/love-and-attachment-haidt.md)** — Haidt's synthesis of attachment theory and positive psychology

## The Happiness Hypothesis: Ten Core Ideas

| Idea | Ancient source | Modern finding |
|------|---------------|----------------|
| The divided self | Plato's chariot, Buddha | Dual-process cognition; the elephant drives |
| Changing your mind | Stoics, Buddhism | CBT works; but the elephant learns slowly |
| Reciprocity | Confucius, golden rule | Reciprocity is universal and pre-rational |
| Hypocrisy and self-deception | Most traditions | Motivated reasoning; WEIRD moral psychology |
| The pursuit of happiness | Epicurus, JS Mill | Adaptation; set point; conditions matter more than outcomes |
| Love and attachment | Aristotle, Christianity | Attachment theory; secure base; need to belong |
| Adversity and growth | Nietzsche, many | Post-traumatic growth is real; optimal challenge |
| The felicity of virtue | Aristotle | Character strengths predict wellbeing; virtues are real |
| Divinity and transcendence | All traditions | Elevation, awe, and moral beauty produce positive emotion |
| Meaning and purpose | Aristotle's *eudaimonia* | Meaning = love + work + connection to something larger |

## Connections

- **[Daniel Kahneman](../people/daniel-kahneman.md)** — Haidt's rider/elephant is Kahneman's System 1/System 2; both show the limits of rational deliberation; Kahneman focuses on error, Haidt on the implications for wellbeing and morality
- **[Mihaly Csikszentmihalyi](../people/mihaly-csikszentmihalyi.md)** — Haidt's "vital engagement" builds on Csikszentmihalyi's flow; both are eudaimonic happiness researchers
- **[Naval Ravikant](../people/naval-ravikant.md)** — Naval's subtractive happiness (remove desire) vs. Haidt's additive happiness (build conditions); complementary, not contradictory
- **[Daniel Goleman](../people/daniel-goleman.md)** — both work in the territory of emotional intelligence and self-awareness; Haidt explains *why* the elephant resists training; Goleman explains *how* to train it
- **[Clayton Christensen](../people/clayton-christensen.md)** — *How Will You Measure Your Life?* and Haidt's meaning framework converge: relationships + purposeful work + connection to something larger
- **[Robert Cialdini](../people/robert-cialdini.md)** — Cialdini's influence principles operate via the elephant; Haidt's framework explains why they work at a deeper psychological level
