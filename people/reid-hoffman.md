# Reid Hoffman

Co-founder and executive chairman of LinkedIn; partner at Greylock Partners. One of the most influential figures in Silicon Valley's network economy.

## Background

- **PayPal Mafia:** Early executive at PayPal (2000-2002)
- **LinkedIn:** Co-founded in 2003, scaled to 750M+ users, sold to Microsoft for $26B (2016)
- **Greylock Partners:** Joined 2009; invested in Airbnb, Facebook, Dropbox, Instagram, others
- **Board member:** Microsoft, Zynga (past), Convoy, others

## Core Philosophy

Hoffman applies startup thinking to everything: companies, products, and **careers**. His central insight is that in an era of constant change, individuals must adopt the same adaptive, iterative, risk-intelligent strategies that successful startups use.

## Key Ideas

### The Startup Mindset Applied to Careers

See [The Start-up of You](../sources/hoffman-startup-of-you.md) for full framework:

- **[Permanent beta](../concepts/permanent-beta.md):** You are never finished; always evolving
- **[ABZ planning](../concepts/abz-planning.md):** Plan A (current path), Plan B (pivot), Plan Z (lifeboat)
- **[Competitive Advantage](../concepts/competitive-advantage.md)** from Assets + Aspirations + Market realities intersection
- **[Network intelligence](../concepts/network-intelligence.md):** Your network is your career capital
- **I^We:** Individual power comes from collective network ([I^We concept](../concepts/i-we.md))

### Networks as Leverage

Hoffman's entire career has been about building and leveraging networks:
- LinkedIn productizes professional networks
- Invested in network-effect businesses (marketplaces, social platforms)
- Argues that [weak ties](../concepts/weak-ties.md) (acquaintances) are more valuable than strong ties for career opportunities

**Key insight:** Information, opportunities, and resources flow through networks. Your value is partly determined by the strength and diversity of your network.

### Intelligent Risk-Taking

- Distinguish **volatility** (short-term ups and downs) from **risk** (permanent loss)
- Take more volatility early in career when you can recover
- Always have Plan Z (safety net) so you can take bigger swings with Plan A and B
- The "safe" path (stability without growth) is actually risky in a changing world

## Relation to Other Thinkers

- **[Naval Ravikant](naval-ravikant.md):** Both emphasize building [leverage](../concepts/leverage.md), [specific knowledge](../concepts/specific-knowledge.md), and long-term [compounding](../concepts/compounding.md). Hoffman focuses more on networks as leverage; Naval on code/media/capital.
- **[Clayton Christensen](clayton-christensen.md):** Applies [Jobs to Be Done](../concepts/jobs-to-be-done.md) thinking to careers — what job is the market hiring you to do?
- **[Nassim Taleb](nassim-taleb.md):** [ABZ planning](../concepts/abz-planning.md) mirrors Taleb's [barbell strategy](../concepts/barbell-strategy.md) — safe base + aggressive upside.
- **[Mark Granovetter](mark-granovetter.md):** Builds directly on Granovetter's [weak ties](../concepts/weak-ties.md) research.
- **[Peter Thiel](peter-thiel.md):** Both PayPal alumni; shared emphasis on network effects and contrarian thinking, but Hoffman is more collaborative/networked while Thiel emphasizes monopoly/competition.

## Key Works

- **The Start-up of You** (2012, with [Ben Casnocha](ben-casnocha.md)) — Applying startup principles to career development
- **The Alliance** (2014, with Casnocha and Chris Yeh) — Redefining employer-employee relationship as "tour of duty"
- **Blitzscaling** (2018, with Chris Yeh) — How to scale companies at lightning speed; prioritizing speed over efficiency
- **Masters of Scale** podcast — Interviews with founders on how they scaled their companies

## Key Contributions

1. **Made professional networking systematic:** LinkedIn turned networking from an art into a platform
2. **Startup thinking → career strategy:** Popularized applying startup frameworks (pivoting, iterating, investing) to personal career
3. **Weak ties theory → practice:** Took Granovetter's academic research and made it actionable
4. **Network effects investing:** Pattern-matched network effects across industries (social, marketplace, platform)

## Why He Matters in This Wiki

Hoffman represents the synthesis of:
- **Strategy** (competitive advantage, differentiation, positioning)
- **Networks** (information flow, relationship capital, weak ties)
- **Career development** (building leverage, adaptive planning, intelligent risk)
- **Silicon Valley wisdom** (startup thinking, growth mindset, compounding)

His frameworks connect to nearly every domain in this wiki: from [systems thinking](../concepts/systems-thinking.md) (network effects are [Feedback Loops](../concepts/feedback-loops.md)) to [personal development](../pages/personal-development.md) (permanent beta) to [strategy](../pages/strategy-frameworks.md) (competitive advantage).
