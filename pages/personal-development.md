# Personal Development Frameworks

A navigational map of the personal development content in this wiki. These frameworks answer: *how do you build a good career, do great work, and live a meaningful life?*

---

## The Core Tension

Most personal development advice pulls in two directions:

**Extrinsic track:** Optimise for output, status, and external validation. Chase credentials, titles, salary. Measure success by what others can see.

**Intrinsic track:** Optimise for genuine capability, meaning, and internal satisfaction. Build specific knowledge, do deep work, pursue autotelic engagement.

The wiki's content consistently supports the intrinsic track — not as a moral claim, but as an empirical one. [Christensen](../people/clayton-christensen.md) (Herzberg), [Csikszentmihalyi](../people/mihaly-csikszentmihalyi.md) (flow), and [Naval](../people/naval-ravikant.md) (specific knowledge) all arrive at the same place from different directions: extrinsic optimisation produces a hollow career; intrinsic engagement produces compounding capability and genuine satisfaction.

## The Compounding Stack

These frameworks fit together as a compounding system:

```
Specific Knowledge (Naval)
    ↑
Built through Deep Work (Newport)
    ↑
Which requires Flow conditions (Csikszentmihalyi)
    ↑
Which requires Autotelic engagement with the right work
    ↑
Identified through: What is your metric? (Christensen)
    ↑
Sustained by OKRs + deliberate goal-setting (Doerr)
    ↑
Compounded over time through long-term games (Naval)
```

The insight: these aren't separate frameworks — they're layers of the same system. Deep work is the *practice* that generates flow; flow is the *phenomenology* of building specific knowledge; specific knowledge is what compounds into leverage; leverage is what makes the work matter economically.

**EQ as the substrate:** Goleman's emotional intelligence sits *beneath* all of this. Self-regulation enables deep work (managing distraction and discomfort); self-awareness enables honest goal-setting; empathy and social skills determine whether your compounding happens alone or with and through others — which dramatically changes the ceiling.

## Career Diagnosis

**"Why does my career feel hollow despite external success?"**
→ [HWYMYL (Herzberg)](../concepts/how-will-you-measure-your-life.md): you've been optimising for hygiene factors (salary, title) rather than motivators (challenge, growth, meaning). The hygiene factors remove pain; only motivators create genuine satisfaction.

**"Why am I always busy but never feel like I'm making progress?"**
→ [Deep Work vs Shallow Work](../concepts/shallow-work.md): most of your time is shallow — visible, urgent, and low-value. The valuable work requires a different mode entirely.

**"How do I build skills that compound?"**
→ [Specific Knowledge + Deep Work](../concepts/specific-knowledge.md): genuine curiosity + distraction-free concentration + time = irreplaceable capability. Can't be shortcut.

**"How do I stay consistent on things that matter?"**
→ [OKRs + Commitment & Consistency](../concepts/okrs.md): clear goals + public accountability + lead measure tracking. Structure beats willpower.

**"How do I know if I'm making the right choices over a lifetime?"**
→ [HWYMYL](../concepts/how-will-you-measure-your-life.md): decide on your metric now. The deathbed metric is depth of relationships and lives touched — not revenue or status. Choose deliberately, not by default.

## The Integrity Thread

[Christensen](../concepts/how-will-you-measure-your-life.md), [Taleb](../concepts/skin-in-the-game.md), and [Cialdini](../concepts/commitment-and-consistency.md) converge on a single insight about integrity:

**100% commitment is easier than 98%.** The first exception reshapes identity ("I'm someone who makes exceptions"). Subsequent exceptions become consistent with the new identity. By exception five, the exceptions are the pattern.

The structural defence: decide in advance, in calm conditions, that certain lines will never be crossed. Not "probably not" — never. This eliminates the decision space where integrity erodes. Cialdini provides the mechanism; Christensen provides the life-scale consequence; Taleb provides the accountability design.
