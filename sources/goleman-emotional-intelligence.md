# *Emotional Intelligence* — Daniel Goleman (1995)

## Bibliographic Info

- **Author:** Daniel Goleman
- **Published:** 1995, Bantam Books
- **Genre:** Popular psychology / business
- **Tagline:** *Why It Can Matter More Than IQ*

## Central Argument

IQ and technical skill are threshold competencies — necessary but not sufficient for star performance. What distinguishes the best from the average in most roles is **Emotional Intelligence**: the ability to know and manage your own emotions, and to recognise and influence the emotions of others.

## Key Ideas

1. The **two minds** (emotional vs rational) — and how they interact or collide
2. The **amygdala hijack** — how the emotional brain can override rational thought
3. The **five domains of EQ** — self-awareness, self-regulation, motivation, empathy, social skills
4. EQ is **learnable** — unlike IQ, it can be developed throughout life
5. Emotional literacy in childhood (Goleman advocated for SEL — Social and Emotional Learning — in schools)

## Impact

- 5 million+ copies sold; translated into 40+ languages
- Sparked a movement in corporate L&D around "soft skills"
- Influenced how leadership assessments and hiring work globally
