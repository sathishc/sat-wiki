# "How People Get Rich Now" — Paul Graham (2021)

## Bibliographic Info

- **Author:** Paul Graham
- **Published:** April 2021
- **Origin:** paulgraham.com essay

## Central Argument

The way people get rich has fundamentally shifted in the past 40 years. Inheritance dominated in 1982; by 2020 it was startup founders. Technology collapsed the capital requirements for starting a company to near-zero, making it possible for smart, determined individuals to create enormous value quickly. The rich list is now a record of who built the most useful things.

## Key Ideas

1. **The inheritance era is over.** In 1982, 60 of the 100 richest Americans inherited their wealth. By 2020 that number had fallen dramatically; founders dominate.
2. **Technology collapsed startup costs.** You used to need millions to start a company. Now you need a laptop. This democratised access to company-founding and therefore to wealth creation.
3. **Startups are leverage.** A startup is a way to compress decades of normal career earnings into a few years — if you build something many people want. The mechanism is leverage: your effort is multiplied by technology, not constrained by the hours you work.
4. **Wealth reflects value created.** The new wealthy got rich by building things people wanted — Google, Microsoft, Amazon, Facebook — not by extracting value. This is different from much historical wealth.
5. **The shift is structural, not cyclical.** It's not that the economy is more financialised or more unequal for cyclical reasons. The underlying mechanism — low startup costs, winner-take-most dynamics in technology — is permanent.
6. **Implication: anyone can potentially found.** The wealth barrier to entry has collapsed. The remaining barriers are mostly knowledge, judgment, and will.

## Notable Quotes

> "I came back to this question after reading a 2020 Forbes article about 'the wealth gap.' It said that much of the increase in inequality was due to tech founders getting so rich. And that struck me as slightly odd, because the obvious way to get rich nowadays is to start a successful startup."

> "In 1982, there were 17 billionaires in America. Today there are about 700. Were there really 40x more people living in some kind of uniquely favored circumstances in 2020 than in 1982? No. The difference is startup culture."

> "The way to become rich is to start your own company. The less capital you need, the more accessible that becomes."
