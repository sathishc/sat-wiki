# *Primal Leadership* — Goleman, Boyatzis & McKee (2002)

## Bibliographic Info

- **Authors:** Daniel Goleman, Richard Boyatzis, Annie McKee
- **Published:** 2002, Harvard Business School Press
- **Genre:** Leadership / applied psychology

## Central Argument

The **primal task of leadership** is emotional: to drive the collective emotions of a group in a positive direction, and to clear away the emotional toxicity that blocks performance. Leaders who manage their own emotions and connect with others' feelings create *resonance*; those who don't create *dissonance*.

## Key Ideas

1. **Emotional contagion** — the leader's mood is literally contagious; it sets the emotional climate
2. **Resonant vs dissonant leadership** — resonant leaders attune to others and energise; dissonant ones are out of sync and drain
3. **Six leadership styles** (each with an EQ basis):

| Style | When to Use | EQ Driver |
|-------|------------|-----------|
| Visionary | Change; new direction | Self-confidence, empathy |
| Coaching | Develop long-term strengths | Empathy, self-awareness |
| Affiliative | Heal rifts; motivate in stress | Empathy, social skills |
| Democratic | Build consensus; get buy-in | Collaboration, communication |
| Pacesetting | Quick results from motivated team | Achievement drive |
| Commanding | Crisis; turn around | Initiative, self-control |

4. **Four EQ clusters** (refinement of the original five): Self-Awareness, Self-Management, Social Awareness, Relationship Management
5. **Renewal** — leaders must manage their own emotional energy to avoid burnout
